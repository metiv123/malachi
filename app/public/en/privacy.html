Privacy Notice

Version 2026-08-02 · UK pilot

Who is responsible for the information?

Angelo is operated during the pilot by Metiv Shlomo Hadar in Israel. Privacy, access, correction and deletion requests can be sent to Metiv.shlomo@gmail.com or WhatsApp +972 55-263-9584.

What we collect

  • The family account holder’s name, email, telephone number and password hash.
  • The older person’s and alert contacts’ names and WhatsApp numbers.
  • Check-in time, time zone, reminder settings and consent status.
  • Message delivery, response and no-response records.
  • Support messages, security logs and referral/UTM data when present.
  • Aggregate site usage such as page views, important button clicks and signup stages.

Angelo does not ask for medical information. Please do not enter medical or other sensitive information that is not needed.

Why we use it

We use the information to create and secure the account, send the requested WhatsApp check-ins and alerts, provide the family dashboard, prevent misuse, solve faults, understand which outreach works and improve the pilot. Marketing messages are optional and require separate consent.

Information about another person

The account holder must talk to the older person and alert contacts before adding them. Angelo sends each person a direct WhatsApp approval request. Routine messages start only after the relevant approval is received, and consent can be refused or withdrawn.

Service providers and international processing

Information is processed only as needed by the authorised family and by providers used to run the service, including Meta/WhatsApp, Google/Firebase and Render. Processing may take place in Israel, the United States and other countries where these providers operate. Angelo does not sell personal information.

Automated process

The system automatically checks whether a WhatsApp response was received within the configured period. It may then send a reminder or a family alert. This is not a medical decision or a safety assessment.

Privacy-friendly site measurement

We count page views, important clicks, referral/UTM sources and signup stages in aggregate. We do not store raw IP addresses or build personal visitor profiles. A short-lived privacy-preserving statistical sketch is used to estimate unique visitors. Browsers with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled do not send these events. No advertising pixel is used.

How long we keep it

  • Account and settings: while the account is active.
  • Checks, messages and delivery status: up to 180 days.
  • Aggregate anonymous usage measurements: up to 90 days.
  • Security, support and error logs: up to 365 days.
  • Waiting-list records: up to 180 days.
  • Local backups: up to 30 days, subject to deletion filtering.

Your choices and rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion, withdraw optional marketing consent, or stop WhatsApp messages. We may request reasonable identity verification. UK residents may also have rights under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, including the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

The service uses an essential secure login cookie and local storage for the privacy notice. Aggregate measurement does not set an advertising or analytics cookie.

Security and contact

We use encrypted transport, password hashing, protected sessions, rate limits, access controls and retention rules. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Contact Metiv.shlomo@gmail.com with any concern.
