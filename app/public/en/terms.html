What Malachi does

Malachi sends a scheduled WhatsApp check-in to a consenting adult. When “I’m okay” is received, the result is recorded and the family is not routinely notified. If no response is received after the configured reminders, approved family contacts may receive an alert.

Not an emergency or medical service

Malachi detects only a WhatsApp response. It does not detect health, consciousness, location, falls, danger or need for help. A response is not proof that a person is safe. No response may be caused by a flat battery, incorrect number, connection problem, blocked message or provider fault. Do not rely on Malachi as the only way to protect a person. In urgent circumstances, contact the person directly and call the appropriate emergency service.
